Features

Everything your meme folder wishes it could do

Panel 1 — Search

Search by what the meme says

MemeFactory reads the text in every image, GIF and video when you add it. Type “monday” and every Monday meme shows up, even if the file is called IMG_4031.png.

Panel 3 — Share

Drag it into the chat

Drag any meme straight into Discord, WhatsApp Web, Slack or a post, or hit Copy and paste. GIFs arrive animated.

Panel 4 — GIFs & videos

Moving pictures welcome

GIFs, animated WebPs and short videos (MP4, MOV, WebM) live next to your images. They play on hover, and their captions are searchable too.

Panel 5 — Organise

Tags, sorting and dates

Tag memes, filter to just GIFs or just videos, sort by newest or by name, or find everything you added this week.

Panel 6 — Meme Studio

Make new memes in the built-in Studio

Classic top and bottom text, caption boxes, stickers and shapes. Cut anyone out of a photo with the lasso, stack layers, and save straight into your library, already searchable.

Comes with 64 original stickers and characters, plus packs and Creative Commons search.

Mac says it “can’t be opened”?

The app isn’t notarized by Apple yet. Right-click MemeFactory in Applications → Open → Open. Or run this once in Terminal:

xattr -cr /Applications/MemeFactory.app

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. MemeFactory is open source under the MIT licence. No trials, no ads, no “pro” tier.

Does it upload my memes anywhere?

No. Your library, the text it reads and your tags stay on your computer. The app only goes online when you paste a link or browse the optional online sticker catalog.

Which Mac download do I need?

Apple menu → About This Mac. If the chip says Apple M…, get Apple Silicon. If it says Intel, get Intel.

Can it read memes in other languages?

Text recognition works best with English captions today. Titles and tags work in any language.

Why can’t some Instagram videos be saved?

Instagram shows some videos only to logged-in users. MemeFactory never asks for your accounts, so for those, download the video another way and drop the file in.

I found a bug or have an idea

Open an issue on GitHub. Feature ideas are very welcome.
