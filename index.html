Under the hood

How the sausage gets made

Memes go into the scanner, words come out, and everything lands in a tiny database on your disk. The cloud was not invited.

  • Reads PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, BMP and video frames
  • Indexes into SQLite full-text search, on your disk
  • Uploads 0 bytes. Zero. Zilch. Nada.

Mac says “MemeFactory can’t be opened” 💀

Apple hasn’t notarized us yet (we’re still doing the paperwork). Right-click MemeFactory in Applications → Open → Open. Or paste this into Terminal once and feel like a hacker:

xattr -cr /Applications/MemeFactory.app

Windows says “Windows protected your PC” 🙄

The installer isn’t code-signed yet (certificates cost money and this app costs nothing). Click More info → Run anyway. That’s it, no hacking required.

FAQ

Questions you were too afraid to ask

“Will you upload my memes?”No.
Is it actually free?

Yes. Free, open source, MIT licence. No trial, no ads, no “Pro” tier hiding the good stuff. It’s free real estate.

Are you uploading my memes?

Nope. Your library, the text it reads and your tags never leave your computer. It only goes online when you paste a link or browse the optional sticker catalog. Your cursed memes are safe with you.

Which Mac one do I need?

Apple menu → About This Mac. If the chip says Apple M…, get Apple Silicon. If it says Intel, get Intel. Bought your Mac after 2020? It’s almost certainly Apple Silicon.

Does it read memes in other languages?

Text recognition speaks English best right now. Titles and tags work in any language, emoji included. 🐸

Why won’t some Instagram videos save?

Instagram hides some videos behind a login, and we refuse to ask for your password. For those, download the video another way and drop the file in.

I found a bug / I have a genius idea

Open an issue on GitHub. Bug reports are welcome, feature ideas even more. Meme submissions… also welcome, honestly.
